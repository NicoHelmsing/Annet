Who is responsible?

{{NAME}}, {{LOCATION}}, the Netherlands. You can reach me at {{EMAIL}}.

What do I collect?

Only what you fill in yourself when you send me a message through the contact form or by email:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • your phone number, if you give it (it is optional);
  • your message.

I never ask for more than I need. There are no accounts and no online payments.

What do I use it for?

Only to answer your question, for example about buying a work, a commission or a studio visit, and to make arrangements about it. I do this because you asked me to get in touch (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). I do not send newsletters or advertising, and I never sell your details.

How long do I keep it?

I keep your message and details for no longer than one year after our last contact, unless you buy a work or commission one. Details needed for the records of a sale are kept for as long as the law requires (seven years in the Netherlands).

Who helps me with this?

This website uses a few services from other companies:

  • Web3Forms (Web3Creative, India) forwards your message from the contact form to my email. Web3Forms also stores submitted messages itself, for up to three years, and checks them for spam. For this, your email address and IP address are also checked by their spam filters (including in the United States). For transfers outside the European Union, Web3Forms uses the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, part of Microsoft, United States) hosts this website. As with any website, the server receives your IP address in order to show you the page; GitHub keeps it for a short time for security. GitHub takes part in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • My email provider, where your message arrives in my mailbox.

If you email me directly instead of using the form, your message does not pass through Web3Forms.

Cookies and statistics

This website sets no cookies and uses no statistics or advertising services. No fonts or other files are loaded from Google. If you switch the language to English, your own browser remembers that choice. The setting stays on your device and is not sent to me or anyone else.

Security

The website uses a secure connection (https), so what you type into the form is sent encrypted.

Your rights

You can always ask me which of your details I have, ask me to correct or delete them, and object to their use. Just email {{EMAIL}}. I will reply within one month.

If you are not satisfied with how I handle your details, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

If something about this website changes, I will update this statement. The date at the top shows when that last happened.
